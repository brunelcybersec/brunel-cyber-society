Stay Safe Online

  
New laptop, new uni email, new network to join, freshers week hands you a lot of new accounts at once. Here's what's actually worth doing about it, starting with six things you can finish before your next lecture.






  

    //
    
Do these six things today

  

  

    

      

        
          
        
      

      
Get a password manager

      
One strong password per account, not one password for everything.

    

    

      

        
          
        
      

      
Turn on 2FA

      
Start with your email and anything involving money.

    

    

      

        
          
        
      

      
Update your stuff

      
Phone, laptop, browser. Don't dismiss the update prompt forever.

    

    

      

        
          
        
      

      
Slow down before you click

      
Unexpected link in an email or text? Go to the site yourself instead.

    

    

      

        
          
        
      

      
Lock your screen

      
Every time you step away from a library or campus computer.

    

    

      

        
          
        
      

      
Back up your coursework

      
OneDrive, Google Drive, whatever, just not only on one laptop.
